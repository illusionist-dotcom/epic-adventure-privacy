Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

Illusionist World (“we,” “our,” or “us”) develops Epic Adventure, an offline single-player adventure game. This policy describes how the game handles information.

Information and accounts

Epic Adventure does not require an account, registration, or submission of personal information to play. The current game does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party crash-reporting integrations.

Game progress and local storage

The game stores gameplay progress and settings locally on your device. Epic Adventure does not operate its own cloud synchronization service. Depending on your device configuration and Android backup settings, some app data may be backed up or restored by the operating system or your device provider.

Internet access and third parties

Epic Adventure is designed for offline play and does not require an internet connection for normal gameplay. The current game does not offer multiplayer, in-app purchases, or online accounts. Device operating systems and distribution platforms may independently process information under their own policies.

Children's privacy

Epic Adventure does not ask players to provide personal information or create accounts. If you have a question about a child's privacy while using the game, please contact us.

Data retention and deletion

Game progress and settings remain on your device unless removed through the game's available controls, the device's app-data settings, or uninstalling the app. Data restored from device backups may be subject to your operating system or backup provider's retention rules.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when game features or data practices change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Illusionist World at mentalengineering.games@gmail.com.
